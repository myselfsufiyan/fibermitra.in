Airtel Xstream Fiber or Airtel AirFiber?

Both give you Airtel Wi-Fi at home. Which one you get depends on whether fiber has reached your building.

Airtel Xstream Fiber

A fiber line is brought into your home and connected to the router. This is the wired Airtel broadband service, with speeds from 40 Mbps up to 1 Gbps on the plans above.

See Xstream Fiber plans

Airtel AirFiber

Home Wi-Fi delivered wirelessly over the Airtel 5G network, meant for areas where fiber has not been laid yet. No cable is pulled to your home. Availability and plans depend on your address.

Questions people ask

What is the cheapest Airtel Wi-Fi plan?

The ₹499 a month plan. It gives 40 Mbps speed with Google One and Perplexity Pro AI included.

Which Airtel broadband plans include Netflix?

Plans at ₹999 a month and above. They also include Amazon Prime, JioHotstar, ZEE5 and Apple TV+.

Is the router and installation free with Airtel Xstream Fiber?

Yes. The router and installation are free. Free hardware and installation applies with an advance payment of ₹2500.

What is the difference between Airtel Xstream Fiber and Airtel AirFiber?

Xstream Fiber is a wired fiber line brought into your home. AirFiber is home Wi-Fi delivered wirelessly over the Airtel 5G network, meant for areas where fiber has not been laid yet. Availability depends on your address.

Do Airtel Wi-Fi plans come with a landline?

Free landline local and STD calls are available in selected towns across select plans. Ask us and we will check for your town.
